Features of Landscape Recorder:

  • Records portrait videos through a landscape mode "viewfinder" on your iPhone
  • In live concerts and crowded places it is difficult to hold a phone in landscape mode — with the hand in a comfortable position you can record portrait videos that are perfect for landscape conversion
  • Either on the spot or later when you have time you can go into the queue and decide which videos get converted
  • Portrait videos are recorded in 4K ensuring the Landscape Video is at least 1080P quality
  • Imagine vlogging in the field and posting portrait to instagram while posting landscape to youtube?

Learn English for Albanian speakers:

  • Language learning app with no exams - just learn naturally
  • Learn to speak English for Albanians
  • Introductory offer - it's completely free!
  • Future versions will have more features and sentences
  • Proven algorithms that speed up learning and enhance retention
  • Press and hold listen button to turn off sound
  • Press and hold forward/next button to change sentence modes

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

BD Software ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy applies to Towers of H'Annoy, Landscape Recorder, and Gjuhe Angleze.

🌉 Towers of H'Annoy

ZERO data collection. The puzzle app:

  • ✅ No analytics tools
  • ✅ No crash reporters
  • ✅ No advertising identifiers
  • ✅ No account creation
  • ✅ No internet access required
  • ✅ No permissions requested (Camera, Microphone, Photos not used)

All game progress (score, moves, settings) is stored only on your device and never transmitted.

🎬 Landscape Recorder

No information collected. The camera app requires:

  • Camera access — to record video through the viewfinder
  • Microphone access — to capture audio during recording
  • Photos Library access — to save videos and allow conversion

Critical: All recordings stay on-device. We do not upload, transmit, or have any ability to view your photos or videos. No analytics, tracking, or data collection of any kind.

🗣️ Gjuhe Angleze

No data collection. The language learning app:

  • ✅ All learning progress stays on your device
  • ✅ No analytics or tracking
  • ✅ No account required
  • ✅ No internet connection needed for lessons
  • ✅ No personal information collected

Your language learning journey is completely private and offline.

🔒 Unified Privacy Promise

  • ✅ No analytics SDKs in any app
  • ✅ No crash reporters
  • ✅ No advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • ✅ No tracking of any kind
  • ✅ No account sign-up
  • ✅ No server data storage

📱 Local Processing Only

Towers of H'Annoy: All game state stays on your device.
Landscape Recorder: Video conversion happens entirely on your iPhone. No footage ever leaves your device.
Gjuhe Angleze: All language lessons and progress stay on your device.

👶 Children's Privacy

All apps are suitable for all ages and do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

📝 Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date while maintaining our zero-data commitment.
